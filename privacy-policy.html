Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NEMAJ APPS TECHNOLOGIES handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us or engage our services.

1. Who we are

“NEMAJ APPS TECHNOLOGIES”, “we”, “us” or “our” refers to the business providing the software development, web design and intranet development services described on this website.

Our principal business activities are software development, web design and intranet development. This website provides information about those services and allows visitors to prepare an email enquiry.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • identity and contact information such as your name, email address, telephone number and organisation;
  • project enquiry details, requirements, files or other information you choose to send us;
  • service, invoice, payment status and transaction-reference information needed to manage an engagement, support request, refund or payment dispute;
  • basic technical information made available by your browser or website hosting provider, such as IP address, device/browser type, pages requested and server logs.

The enquiry form prepares a message in the visitor’s email application; it does not submit the form contents to or store them on this website. Please do not send card numbers, PINs, CVVs, passwords or other highly sensitive authentication information by email.

3. How we use information

We use relevant information to respond to enquiries, assess project requirements, prepare quotations, enter into and perform service agreements, communicate about delivery, provide customer support, manage invoices and payment records, handle refunds or chargebacks, maintain website operations, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We process personal information only where there is an appropriate lawful basis, such as taking steps at your request before a contract, performing a contract, meeting a legal obligation, pursuing a legitimate business interest or obtaining your consent where required.

4. When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate our business, such as website hosting, business email, software tools, payment service providers and professional advisers. We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect rights or property, or to investigate suspected fraud or security incidents.

Where a payment service provider processes a payment, that provider may handle payment information under its own privacy and security terms. We do not ask customers to send full card details through this website.

5. Retention and security

We keep personal information for only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including applicable accounting, contract, dispute-resolution and legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of certain personal information, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority.

To make a request, contact us using the support email below and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will handle requests in line with applicable data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 where it applies.

7. Cookies and analytics

This website does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics by default. Our hosting provider may create essential technical logs to deliver and protect the website. If analytics, advertising pixels, embedded media or other non-essential tracking tools are added later, this policy and any consent mechanism required by law must be updated before those tools are activated.

8. Links to other websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or platforms. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review their privacy notices before providing information to them.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, website or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
