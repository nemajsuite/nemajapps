Refund Policy

This policy explains how customers can request a refund or cancellation for software development, web design and intranet development services supplied by NEMAJ APPS TECHNOLOGIES.

Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to project-based software development, web design and intranet development services supplied by NEMAJ APPS TECHNOLOGIES. An accepted quotation or service agreement may contain additional project-specific cancellation and refund terms. Where the law gives a customer a right that cannot be excluded, that right continues to apply.

2. When a refund may be considered

We review each request fairly and in light of the agreed scope, work completed, payment records and applicable law. A refund may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • a duplicate payment or an amount charged in error;
  • a customer cancels before project work has started, subject to any unavoidable third-party costs that were clearly disclosed and lawfully payable;
  • we are unable to supply a material part of the agreed service and, after a reasonable opportunity to correct the issue or agree another solution, the issue remains unresolved;
  • a project agreement expressly provides for a refund at a particular milestone or on a stated event.

3. Circumstances where a full refund may not apply

To the extent permitted by law and the applicable agreement, a full refund may not be available for:

  • work already completed and delivered in accordance with the agreed scope;
  • milestones or deliverables reviewed and accepted by the customer;
  • custom work already performed, where the relevant fee was properly agreed and earned;
  • third-party licences, hosting, domain, marketplace or other external costs that were disclosed before purchase and cannot be recovered;
  • delays or inability to proceed caused by missing customer content, access, approvals or other customer responsibilities, except where the agreement or law provides otherwise.

Where only part of a project remains incomplete, we may consider a proportionate refund or another reasonable resolution based on the undelivered portion and the project agreement.

4. How to request a refund

Send your request to our support email or chargeback/payment support email below. Include:

  • your full name and the email address used for the project;
  • the project, service or invoice reference;
  • the payment date, amount and transaction reference (do not include your full card number, PIN or CVV);
  • the reason for the request and the outcome you are seeking;
  • relevant correspondence or evidence that may help us review the request.

For your protection, contact us from the email address associated with the project where possible. We may request reasonable information to confirm the transaction and your authority to make the request.

5. Review and refund processing

We will acknowledge and review a complete request within a reasonable period. We may contact you for clarification or supporting information. If a refund is approved, we will normally initiate it to the original payment method or another method agreed with you, where permitted by the payment provider and applicable law.

If a refund is approved, we will communicate the decision and arrange the refund to the original payment method or another method agreed with the customer, where permitted. The time for funds to appear may depend on the bank or payment service provider and may be outside our control.

6. Payment disputes and chargebacks

Please contact us promptly if you do not recognise a charge, believe you paid twice or have a concern about a service. We will review the transaction and attempt to resolve the issue. You retain any rights available through your bank, card issuer or payment provider. Nothing in this policy prevents you from exercising rights provided by law or the applicable payment scheme.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or procedures change. The current version will be published on this page. The policy and project agreement applicable to a payment should be reviewed before making that payment.
