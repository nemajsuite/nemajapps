What services do you provide?

Our principal services are website design and development, custom software development and intranet development. The deliverables for each project are confirmed in writing.

Who can engage your services?

We work with individuals, businesses and organisations that need website, software or intranet development. We review each requirement before confirming whether it fits our services.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity, timeline and any third-party costs. We provide a quotation or proposal for acceptance before work and payment begin.

How do payments and delivery work?

Payment terms, milestones, delivery dates and handover arrangements are stated in the accepted quotation or service agreement. Customers should pay only through the payment instructions officially issued for their project.

Can I request a refund?

Refund requests are reviewed under our Refund Policy and the agreement for the specific project. Contact our support team with your payment reference and project details.

How do you handle my personal information?

We use the information needed to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals and provide agreed services. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.
